# Artificial Intelligence Resources at CSU
\
Artificial intelligence is transforming learning, research, and work. This page brings together key resources at Colorado State University to help faculty, students, and staff explore AI responsibly and effectively.

## Provost AI Working Group Draft Resources

CSU NetID required to access these documents.

[Assessment Guidance – Aug2026 DRAFT.docx](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQBxIzKDaOd3SojixDC2hRXeAVBleW4DDs5_08om8rCh0UE) – A brief guide focused on assessment design as a durable response to AI, including in-class work, process artifacts, local prompts, AI-output critique, oral checks, and accessibility considerations. Useful for faculty revising assignments rather than relying on detection.

[Departmental Guidance – Aug2026 DRAFT.docx](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQD3ahRC86TbQ61LsvCrdZ9aAbJZVBQmRjUqkkoHLtr8E9k) – Guidance for department heads and chairs on adopting shared AI syllabus statements consistently and defensibly. Useful for departments coordinating faculty communication and aligning course policies with CSU conduct and faculty manual language.

[Syllabus statements – Aug2026 DRAFT.docx](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQAcW4hf1_LoTqCRqsv2MtzaAa-dH7NBji7MfdNWgul7U4U) – Three copy-ready syllabus statement tiers cover courses where AI is prohibited, permitted with disclosure, or expected and assessed. Useful for instructors who need clear language they can adapt while preserving required elements like accommodations, “ask me first,” and student responsibility.

**Teaching & Learning with AI**\
--------------------------------

**[AI and Academic Integrity](https://tilt.colostate.edu/ai-and-ai/) (TILT)**\
This page from CSU’s Institute for Learning and Teaching (TILT) explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and academic integrity. It offers guidance for faculty on how to adapt teaching practices in light of generative AI tools, emphasizing ethical use and student learning outcomes.

**[Field Guide to Generative AI](https://wac.colostate.edu/repository/exhibits/a-field-guide-to-effective-generative-ai-use/) (WAC Clearinghouse)**\
Hosted by CSU’s Writing Across the Curriculum Clearinghouse, this exhibit offers a comprehensive field guide for using generative AI effectively in academic settings. It includes practical strategies, ethical frameworks, and examples to support thoughtful integration of AI in writing and learning.\

## Research & Scholarship

**[AI Research Guide](https://libguides.colostate.edu/ai)** **(CSU Libraries)**\
The CSU Libraries provide a curated research guide on artificial intelligence, including resources for understanding AI technologies, ethical considerations, and their impact on research and education. It’s a helpful starting point for students and faculty exploring AI-related topics.

[**CSU Writes AI Guidance**\
](https://csuwrites.colostate.edu/ai-guide/)CSU Writes offers practical frameworks and training to help researchers and scholars make intentional decisions about when (and when not) to integrate generative AI into their writing processes. Aligned with disciplinary guidelines, standards for ethical research conduct, and writing as a mode of professional development, CSU Writes provides faculty, graduate students, and research teams with resources for responsible and effective AI use, including downloadable AI-use templates for co-authors and collaborative teams, as well as AI planning and reflection worksheets for research labs, and more.

## AI Governance Guidelines

[AI Governance Guidelines](https://datagov.csusystem.edu/ai-governance-guidelines/) **(CSU System)**\
Developed by the CSU System Data Governance Committee, this resource outlines institutional guidelines for the ethical, secure, and transparent use of AI across administrative, research, clinical, and educational domains. It emphasizes responsible data stewardship, equity, and human oversight, offering classification-based protocols and principles for AI use.
