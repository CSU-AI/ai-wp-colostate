# CVMBS AI Newsletter #4
### **What You Should Know**

Fiscal year 2025 is shaping up to be the year AI Agents mature into mainstream tools. Unlike traditional chatbots, AI Agents are designed to work autonomously, using external tools and making decisions to complete tasks independently. This distinction from chatbots is crucial for understanding the expanded capabilities and potential impact of this next wave of AI: where a chatbot will explain how to schedule a team meeting, an agent will check all calendars, identify optimal times, send invites, reserve conference rooms, and manage follow-ups. ### **AI** **In Education**

- [A report by EDUCAUSE](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.umass.edu%2Fideas%2Fdigest%2Fai-wake-call-universities-key-insights-2025-educause-survey&data=05%7C02%7CDavid.Edwards%40colostate.edu%7C07fa579db68e4dc27b4d08ddb5a7e4dc%7Cafb58802ff7a4bb1ab21367ff2ecfc8b%7C0%7C0%7C638866454882024769%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=DHKCm3h2oktKbQXqo7Xu4J4X5PgxUkz0JLfm85nqoJo%3D&reserved=0) states that **86% of college students regularly use AI** tools in their studies, with 59% expecting their university to increase the technology’s use in teaching and learning. Over 80% of faculty reported using AI for at least one work-related task, although **66% of universities reported having no institution-wide AI strategy**.
- [More than half of higher education leaders cite a lack of faculty familiarity](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fedtechmagazine.com%2Fhigher%2Farticle%2F2025%2F05%2Fcolleges-and-universities-offer-faculty-development-ai-use-classroom&data=05%7C02%7CDavid.Edwards%40colostate.edu%7C07fa579db68e4dc27b4d08ddb5a7e4dc%7Cafb58802ff7a4bb1ab21367ff2ecfc8b%7C0%7C0%7C638866454882038209%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=eMc1Wpx4%2BOA%2BkHeJ7KJfdSHb3I7%2F4kI7gKT%2FGHrOB7U%3D&reserved=0) with AI tools as a challenge to adoption. Colleges are increasingly **offering faculty development opportunities for AI** use in the classroom, including University of Texas at Austin, University of Central Florida, and Vanderbilt University.
- [The World Economic Forum emphasized](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.weforum.org%2Fstories%2F2025%2F05%2Fwhy-ai-literacy-is-now-a-core-competency-in-education%2F&data=05%7C02%7CDavid.Edwards%40colostate.edu%7C07fa579db68e4dc27b4d08ddb5a7e4dc%7Cafb58802ff7a4bb1ab21367ff2ecfc8b%7C0%7C0%7C638866454882051462%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=smzGsGaayaCh56XrXny1Z8MrwT1BhrToVXhXdWX6XLA%3D&reserved=0) that **AI literacy is becoming a core competency in education**, beyond traditional digital literacy. A significant portion of Gen Z scores poorly on evaluating and identifying shortfalls in AI technology, such as hallucinations, and sees ethics as a low priority.
- [Harvard has begun directly incorporating generative AI into some of its courses](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcs.harvard.edu%2Fmalan%2Fpublications%2FV1fp0567-liu.pdf&data=05%7C02%7CDavid.Edwards%40colostate.edu%7C07fa579db68e4dc27b4d08ddb5a7e4dc%7Cafb58802ff7a4bb1ab21367ff2ecfc8b%7C0%7C0%7C638866454882064453%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=aeoEhf2BhKkkghWve8wgAte6ycbujiA45%2FHOIfihQSk%3D&reserved=0). An **AI-based tutor was developed** for their introductory computer science course to assist students while **maintaining pedagogical guardrails to guide students and preventing direct answers**. University of Toronto has done similar with a 24/7 “All Day TA”, reportedly handling 12,000 student queries annually.

### **AI In Research**

- [Researchers are sharply divided](https://www-nature-com.ezproxy2.library.colostate.edu/articles/d41586-025-01463-8) on the ethical acceptability of AI use in academic writing and peer review. **90% agree that AI use for editing and translating papers is acceptable**, while a smaller majority of 65% consider text generation acceptable. Often, **researchers will not disclose they had used AI**, further raising the issue of AI-use transparency.

The bill now moves to the senate.
- [An MIT AI Energy Use Review](https://nam10.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.technologyreview.com%2F2025%2F05%2F20%2F1116327%2Fai-energy-usage-climate-footprint-big-tech%2F&data=05%7C02%7CDavid.Edwards%40colostate.edu%7C07fa579db68e4dc27b4d08ddb5a7e4dc%7Cafb58802ff7a4bb1ab21367ff2ecfc8b%7C0%7C0%7C638866454882090614%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=ScWO%2FpBJ%2FTadRIm6IZRoL0rwWVIfK5XI0teAw6SQexA%3D&reserved=0) presents a scientific examination of energy requirements for AI models. ### **Weekly AI Tip**

Need help brainstorming ways to explain complex topics? Prompt your AI with the topic, including your own explanation or supplementary materials, and then instruct it to explain the topic in several different ways. For example: “*Explain the concept of neural networks three times: (a) to a high-school computer science class (b) to a college machine learning student (c) to a senior AIengineer”*

### **AI Spotlight**

**Dr. Camille Torres** has been using ChatGPT as a tool to receive reflective feedback on her lectures and clinical communication. This low-stakes, self-directed method provides specific, unbiased, and constructive insights to improve clarity, organization, tone, and engagement Steps to Get Started:

- Record your lecture, client appointment, or teaching interaction.
- Obtain a transcript (Zoom, Teams, or other transcription tools can help with this).
- Remove identifying information (e.g., client names, patient details).
- Paste the transcript into ChatGPT and request feedback.

#### **Example Prompt:**

***For Clinical Communication (e.g., Appointments):*** *“Please review this client communication transcript. Using the Calgary-Cambridge Guide as a reference, provide feedback on my communication skills, including structure, use of open/closed questions, listening, empathy, and summarization. Also, comment on clarity, tone, and engagement. What worked well, and what are areas I could improve?”*   ***For Teaching (e.g., Lecture or Rounds):*** *“Please analyze this transcript of a lecture I gave to veterinary students. Use principles of effective teaching and communication (e.g., clarity, structure, engagement, use of examples, tone). What did I do well, and what could I improve to better support student learning?”*   *Are you using AI in innovative ways and want to share with your colleagues through this newsletter?
