### **Weekly AI Tip**

Struggling with understanding prompt engineering (the process of crafting your prompts to maximize quality of output)? Try following this simple 4 step guide when writing your prompt:

1.  **Guidance** – What role is the AI filling? What guidelines does it need to follow? What provided information/documentation/data does it need?
2.  **Audience** – Who is the intended output for? Yourself? Your class? A group of professionals? What is their knowledge/skill level?
3.  **Purpose** – Why do you need this? What is the purpose of what you are trying to generate?
4.  **Output** – How do you want your output structured? Do you have a template? Does it need to fit a word count? Does it need to be at a certain reading comprehension level?
