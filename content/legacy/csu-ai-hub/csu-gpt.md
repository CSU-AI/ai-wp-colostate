# CSU-GPT
## Secure, Responsible, and Built for CSU

CSU-GPT gives every student, faculty, and staff member a safe way to explore generative AI—inside CSU’s secure Microsoft Azure environment. It’s powered by [**NebulaOne**](https://ai.colostate.edu/csu-ai-hub/), built on the **Azure OpenAI** stack, and governed by CSU’s own Responsible AI framework.

### A Smarter, Safer AI for CSU

Unlike public AI tools, CSU-GPT keeps all data, prompts, and uploads inside the university’s Microsoft Azure tenant. That means your data never leaves CSU. You get the creative power of GPT-4.0—plus privacy, compliance, and CSU’s ethical use standards.

CSU-GPT can:

- Chat with files (PDFs, docs, spreadsheets)

- Search the web for current, factual information

- Generate ideas, write content, or analyze data

- Create and share **custom agents** built from your own materials

Try CSU-GPT at [**chat.colostate.edu**](https://chat.colostate.edu).

### Create Your Own Agent

**Agents You Can Shape and Share**

Anyone at CSU can create their own AI agent based on a website, document, or dataset. Add instructions, define its purpose, and control who can access it. Agents can be:

- **Private**, for your personal use

- **Shared**, with your team, department, or the CSU Community

Learn about AI Agents (coming soon)

### Why It Matters

**AI for Learning, Collaboration, and Research**

CSU-GPT supports:

- Writing, research, and idea generation

- Teaching and course design

- Administrative process automation

- Responsible AI literacy across campus

Every interaction follows [CSU’s Responsible AI guidelines](https://datagov.csusystem.edu/ai-governance-guidelines/) and data governance standards.
