# Find the Right AI Tool

Start with your task, the data you use, and your role. Narrow your options, then compare the tools that fit your work.

## Explore Your Options

Select up to three tools to compare side by side.

The directory is rendered from the ACF-managed AI Tools post type using `[aicsu_tool_chooser]` in an Elementor Shortcode widget. Review fixtures and implementation are under `wordpress/tool-chooser/`.
