Copilot Chat connected to your own work. Premium puts Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote, and Teams, and grounds its answers in the email, files, chats, calendar, and meetings you already have access to, so you do not need to upload anything. It adds the Researcher and Analyst agents for multi-source reports and data analysis, a choice of OpenAI and Anthropic Claude models, Teams meeting recaps with decisions and action items, and Copilot Notebooks for keeping a project's files and notes in one place.
