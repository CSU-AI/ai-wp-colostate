The default AI chat for everyone at CSU, included with your NetID at no extra cost. Sign in with your CSU account and look for the enterprise data protection shield before you start. Your prompts and responses are covered by CSU's data protection agreement with Microsoft, stay in CSU's tenant, and are not used to train Microsoft's models. Good for writing, summarizing, and asking questions about documents you upload. Web search sends short queries generated from your prompt to Bing, which that agreement does not cover, so turn web search off before working with Level 3 confidential data. If you are not sure which tool to use, start here.
