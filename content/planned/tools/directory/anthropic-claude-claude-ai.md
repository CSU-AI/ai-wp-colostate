A limited pilot of Claude Enterprise with 200 seats, coordinated by the Libraries through a nomination process. Demand already exceeds supply. Two things to understand before you ask for a seat. First, this is the Enterprise license, which bills by actual usage rather than a flat monthly fee, so participants are capped at a $20, $50, or $100 monthly threshold and the cost is recovered from your unit. If you are used to a personal $20 per month plan, the way usage translates into cost will take some adjusting. Second, an existing individual Claude Pro account cannot be moved into the pilot, though accounts can move between Teams and Enterprise. Participants are asked to complete a quarterly feedback survey.
