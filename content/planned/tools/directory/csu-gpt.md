The general purpose chat agent on Nebula One, open to anyone with a CSU NetID at chat.csusystem.edu. Use it for everyday writing, summarizing, and questions when you do not need a specialized agent. Conversations are private and secure, stored in CSU's own Azure tenant and never used to train outside models. If you need an agent grounded in specific documents or connected to a campus system, build one on Nebula One instead of working around CSU-GPT.
