Locally hosted language models can run on your own computer through software such as Ollama or LM Studio. Local-only use can keep prompts and files on your device, but some products also offer cloud models or connected features that send data off-device. CSU has not reviewed or supported this category for confidential or regulated data. Check the model, settings, and security of your computer before using any CSU information.
