Adobe's creative generative AI tool for producing and editing images, vectors, video, and audio on the web and in Creative Cloud apps. Adobe says it does not train Firefly on Creative Cloud subscribers' personal content, but that does not establish CSU approval for institutional data. Use only Level 1 public information, materials you have permission to use, and content you are comfortable sending to Adobe or any selected partner model. Do not upload student records, personnel information, unpublished research, internal documents, or other nonpublic CSU data.
