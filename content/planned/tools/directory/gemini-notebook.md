Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM, turns your sources into summaries, answers with citations, study guides, and other learning materials. CSU students, faculty, and staff can use it with a rams.colostate.edu Google Workspace for Education account for Level 1 public and Level 2 internal data. Sign in with that account before adding CSU sources. A personal Google account is limited to Level 1 public information. Do not add Level 3 confidential or Level 4 restricted data.
