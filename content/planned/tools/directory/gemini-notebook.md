Gemini Notebook, formerly NotebookLM, turns your sources into summaries, answers with citations, study guides, and other learning materials. To use Level 2 internal data, first get a rams.colostate.edu Google account, then sign in with that account before adding CSU sources. The account is optional and free; request one at https://mail.rams.colostate.edu/. A personal Google account is limited to Level 1 public information. Do not add Level 3 confidential or Level 4 restricted data.
