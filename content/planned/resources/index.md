# AI Resource Library

## Purpose

Collect curated guides and reference material that do not belong in tools, teaching, research, or training.

## Required content

- Resources grouped by visitor task rather than source organization
- Brief annotation, audience, owner, and review date for every resource
- Links to FAQ, glossary, and contact
- Filters only if the collection becomes large enough to justify them
