# Using AI With Health Information

## Purpose

Plain-language caveats on entering health information into AI tools, for personal use and for work involving patient, student, or research data.

## Required content

- Do not enter protected health or patient data into tools not approved for it
- Link to [Level 3 data](/tools/level-3-data/) and system data governance rather than restating data tiers
- Accuracy limits of AI for medical questions; when to see a clinician
