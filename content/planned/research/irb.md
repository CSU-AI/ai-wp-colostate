# AI, IRB, and Research Ethics

## Purpose

Direct researchers to the appropriate review when AI affects human-subjects research, consent, privacy, analysis, or participant interaction.

## Required content

- When AI use should be described in a protocol
- Participant data and consent considerations
- Bias, transparency, validation, and human oversight
- IRB consultation and submission links

## Editorial decisions needed

- Do not draft procedural requirements until the IRB approves the content.
