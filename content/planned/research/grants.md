# AI in Grant Applications

## Purpose

Help researchers identify sponsor requirements and document appropriate AI use in proposal development and funded work.

## Required content

- Sponsor disclosure and confidentiality considerations
- Current NSF, NIH, and other major sponsor links
- Human review and accuracy responsibilities
- CSU consultation contacts

## Editorial decisions needed

- Require OVPR review and date-stamp all sponsor-specific claims.
