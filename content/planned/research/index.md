# AI Research Resources

Connect researchers with guidance, computing, tools, and collaborators for responsible AI-enabled research.

## Guides for Researchers and Scholars

**[AI Research Guide](https://libguides.colostate.edu/ai) (CSU Libraries)**

The CSU Libraries provide a curated research guide on artificial intelligence, including resources for understanding AI technologies, ethical considerations, and their impact on research and education. It is a helpful starting point for students and faculty exploring AI-related topics.

**[CSU Writes AI Guidance](https://csuwrites.colostate.edu/ai-guide/)**

CSU Writes offers practical frameworks and training to help researchers and scholars make intentional decisions about when (and when not) to integrate generative AI into their writing processes. Aligned with disciplinary guidelines, standards for ethical research conduct, and writing as a mode of professional development, CSU Writes provides faculty, graduate students, and research teams with resources for responsible and effective AI use, including downloadable AI-use templates for co-authors and collaborative teams, as well as AI planning and reflection worksheets for research labs, and more.
