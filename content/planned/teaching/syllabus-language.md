# AI Syllabus Language

Adaptable language for your syllabus, whether AI is prohibited, permitted with disclosure, or expected and assessed in your course.

## Provost AI Working Group Drafts

CSU NetID required to open these documents. Both are drafts (August 2026).

**[Syllabus statements, Aug 2026 DRAFT (Word)](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQAcW4hf1_LoTqCRqsv2MtzaAa-dH7NBji7MfdNWgul7U4U)**

Three copy-ready syllabus statement tiers cover courses where AI is prohibited, permitted with disclosure, or expected and assessed. Useful for instructors who need clear language they can adapt while preserving required elements like accommodations, "ask me first," and student responsibility.

**[Departmental Guidance, Aug 2026 DRAFT (Word)](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQD3ahRC86TbQ61LsvCrdZ9aAbJZVBQmRjUqkkoHLtr8E9k)**

Guidance for department heads and chairs on adopting shared AI syllabus statements consistently and defensibly. Useful for departments coordinating faculty communication and aligning course policies with CSU conduct and faculty manual language.
