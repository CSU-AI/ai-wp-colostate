# AI Syllabus Language

## Purpose

Provide adaptable CSU language for courses where AI is prohibited, conditionally permitted, or expected and assessed.

## Required content

- Three reviewed syllabus-statement tiers
- Disclosure and citation expectations
- Student responsibility and "ask first" language
- Accessibility and accommodations considerations
- Links to academic integrity policy and teaching consultation

## Editorial decisions needed

- Obtain final approved statements and identify which language is required versus optional.
