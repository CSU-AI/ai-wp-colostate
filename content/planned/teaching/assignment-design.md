# Designing Assignments for AI

## Purpose

Help instructors design durable assessments and productive AI-integrated learning activities.

## Required content

- Start with learning outcomes
- Process evidence, oral checks, local prompts, and AI-output critique
- Alternatives to relying on AI detection
- Examples of prohibited, permitted, and required AI use
- Accessibility and workload considerations
