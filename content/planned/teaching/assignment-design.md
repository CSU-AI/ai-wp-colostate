# Designing Assignments for AI

Design assessments that hold up in a world with AI, and learning activities that use it well.

## Provost AI Working Group Draft

CSU NetID required to open this document. It is a draft (August 2026).

**[Assessment Guidance, Aug 2026 DRAFT (Word)](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:w:/s/Provost_AI_Working_Group/IQBxIzKDaOd3SojixDC2hRXeAVBleW4DDs5_08om8rCh0UE)**

A brief guide focused on assessment design as a durable response to AI, including in-class work, process artifacts, local prompts, AI-output critique, oral checks, and accessibility considerations. Useful for faculty revising assignments rather than relying on detection.
