# Teaching and Learning with AI

Help instructors make intentional, transparent, and learning-centered decisions about AI.

## Start With Your Course

- [AI syllabus language](/teaching/syllabus-language/): statements for courses where AI is prohibited, permitted with disclosure, or expected and assessed.
- [Designing assignments for AI](/teaching/assignment-design/): assessment design as a durable response to AI.

## Campus Guidance

**[AI and Academic Integrity](https://tilt.colostate.edu/ai-and-ai/) (TILT)**

This page from CSU's Institute for Learning and Teaching (TILT) explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and academic integrity. It offers guidance for faculty on how to adapt teaching practices in light of generative AI tools, emphasizing ethical use and student learning outcomes.

**[Field Guide to Generative AI](https://wac.colostate.edu/repository/exhibits/a-field-guide-to-effective-generative-ai-use/) (WAC Clearinghouse)**

Hosted by CSU's Writing Across the Curriculum Clearinghouse, this exhibit offers a comprehensive field guide for using generative AI effectively in academic settings. It includes practical strategies, ethical frameworks, and examples to support thoughtful integration of AI in writing and learning.

## Provost AI Working Group Drafts

Three draft documents from the Provost AI Working Group. A CSU NetID is required to open them, and they are drafts, not final policy. They are described on the pages above:

- Departmental Guidance and Syllabus statements: see [AI syllabus language](/teaching/syllabus-language/)
