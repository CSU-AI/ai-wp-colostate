# Secure the System: AI Training

Recorded sessions from Division of IT on using AI at CSU. Start with Responsible AI at CSU, then follow the order below or jump to the tool or skill you need.

Written for faculty and staff. Students are welcome to watch. Recordings open in Microsoft Stream and are shared with colostate.edu accounts only, so sign in with your CSU NetID.

## Suggested Viewing Order

1. Responsible AI at CSU
2. Understanding Data Governance and AI
3. CSU-GPT Essentials
4. From Prompt to Reviewed Output
5. CSU-GPT Deep Dive
6. Microsoft and Copilot
7. AI Across the CSU System: Panel

Already using AI? Skip to the video that matches your tool. Each recording stands on its own.

## The Recordings

### AI Basics: Responsible AI at CSU

Establishes the university-wide foundation: approved use, data handling, prompting, verification, and practical examples.

[Button: Watch AI Basics recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQCF9K8kt4TdTIOwhh_hMv7LARJ95ghK-2C0Dy8f3-qcKNI?e=X8WDWL)

### AI in Practice: Understanding Data Governance and AI

Learn how data ownership, privacy, access, and governance practices support the responsible and effective use of AI.

[Button: Watch Data Governance and AI recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQBppxwxOMmCQrOERISrYHbZAQdzHLa9b9T4iKS_WGCnmwg?e=LeFKSd)

[Link: Explore CSU System AI Governance Guidelines](https://datagov.csusystem.edu/ai-governance-guidelines/)

### CSU-GPT Essentials: Getting Started

New to CSU-GPT? This hands-on session is your starting point. It walks through everything you need to use CSU-GPT confidently in your day-to-day work, from your first sign-in to your first great prompt. You will leave with practical skills you can apply immediately and a clear understanding of how to use AI responsibly at CSU.

[Button: Watch CSU-GPT Essentials recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQBkLpU8Pt-CS5AmM_K4ugo2ASft0w_oSp2-RlQ1nR2tYoQ?e=8rGGdB)

### AI in Practice: From Prompt to Reviewed Output

Learn a practical approach to writing prompts, refining requests, and reviewing AI-generated responses using the CRAFT method and human judgment.

[Button: Watch Prompt to Reviewed Output recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQAiO8qql4Z9RKtkGym0eB_iASGnCESXDw8aRMyVyapz9Yw?e=r9OvyB)

### CSU-GPT Deep Dive: Tokens and Building Agents

Learn how CSU-GPT uses tokens and context, and explore file and knowledge-source limits and the creation of personal AI agents for complex, repeatable work.

[Button: Watch CSU-GPT Deep Dive recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQBAF_CCu-dRQrNwBaVbkHz2AfXnNi7YM-mAtans5vjAL0Y?e=NUE7SU)

[Link: Browse CSU AI agents](/tools/agents/)

### Microsoft and Copilot

Explore Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Copilot, including Copilot Chat, meeting recaps, prompting tools, Microsoft 365 workflows, data protection, storage, and licensing considerations.

[Button: Watch Microsoft and Copilot recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQBSfwFEAxYUQbY2Fc0O-7RjARt-yUIXj8jvMEJr7T9yWEs?e=8OK84E)

### AI Across the CSU System: Panel

Recording of a panel of four CSU System employees who are using AI in their daily work. Panelists share real examples, lessons learned, successful use cases, and practical tips for getting started, with ideas for improving productivity, supporting decisions, and streamlining routine tasks.

[Button: Watch the panel recording](https://colostate.sharepoint.com/:v:/s/DoIT-_AI-Strategy/IQD2c9CtZHNhRbZq-U3l0wwWAVs5XqK9K3o72TbBVEFhIZU?e=Fj48MB)
