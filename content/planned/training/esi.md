# Microsoft Learn and Enterprise Skills Initiative

Training resources for AI through Microsoft. Use Microsoft Learn for self-paced material and the Enterprise Skills Initiative (ESI) for instructor-led courses.

## How to Access

- **Microsoft Learn:** select a link below to open the training materials.
- **ESI courses:** go to [Microsoft ESI](https://esi.microsoft.com/) and search for the course by title or course number, for example AI-900.

For a full list of AI learning pathways, see the [Microsoft Enterprise Skills Initiative Learning Journey (PDF)](https://ai.colostate.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/45/2025/08/ai-learningPathway.pdf).
