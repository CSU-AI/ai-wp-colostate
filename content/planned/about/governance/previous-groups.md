# Previous AI Groups at CSU

CSU has organized around AI since at least 2024. Two groups completed their work and have ended. Their findings and tools shaped the campus approach described on this site.

For current groups, see [AI Governance at CSU Fort Collins](/about/governance/).

## Enterprise AI Design Team

**Active:** [start date] to December 2025

The Enterprise AI Design Team was a cross-campus group of professionals from the Division of IT and Distributed IT units. It was an 8-month initiative to build the infrastructure, strategy, and skills CSU needed to adopt AI responsibly.

### Mission

Advance CSU's technology environment in support of mission-aligned AI and data analytics goals, such as improving student success. The team also addressed cybersecurity, scalability, and workforce readiness.

### What the team worked on

- **Benchmarking nationally:** aligning CSU's efforts with what works at higher education institutions across the country.
- **Partnering with industry:** working with Microsoft and Amazon to gain access to tools, insights, and best practices.
- **Upskilling staff:** providing training and hands-on experience so CSU IT staff are prepared for AI work.
- **Upgrading infrastructure:** strengthening the technology backbone for AI initiatives and long-term innovation.

### Accomplishments

- Built a prototype of RamGPT, CSU's conversational AI platform.
- Partnered with Microsoft and Cloudforce to co-develop AI strategies.
- Launched hands-on training programs to build AI fluency across IT roles.
- Planned a university-wide launch event for RamGPT.

### How the work happened

The team followed four phases, with weekly half-day working sessions:

1. Discovery and Goal Setting
2. Ideation and Concept Design
3. Development and Prototyping
4. Implementation and Continuous Improvement

## AI Joint Task Force

**Active:** September to December 2024

The AI Joint Task Force brought together experts from CSU Fort Collins and CSU Pueblo to improve how AI practices, processes, and technologies are used across both campuses. Its mission was to build on existing strengths and develop solutions in teaching, research, and operations.

### Focus areas

- Teaching and Learning
- Research
- Productivity
- Communities of Practice
- Innovation

### Outcomes

- Assessment of the current state of AI at CSU
- Pilot program recommendations
- Resource planning
- Risk and privacy management

### Timeline

- **Discovery:** September to mid-October 2024
- **Evaluation:** mid-October to November 2024
- **Recommendations:** December 2024
