# Provost's Artificial Intelligence Working Group

## Overview

In Fall 2026, the Provost's Artificial Intelligence Working Group was charged with guiding how AI, including large language models, is integrated into the university's academic mission. Its focus is on developing policies, tools, practices, and curricula that support effective, ethical, and responsible AI use in teaching, learning, advising, and academic operations.

> The working group will establish a coordinated university-wide strategy for AI that enhances teaching, learning, advising, curriculum development, and academic integrity while ensuring responsible and effective adoption of emerging AI technologies.

The working group will develop guidance and resources for students, faculty, instructors and academic support staff including academic policies and guidelines for AI use; support AI-enabled teaching methods, assessments, learning analytics, and classroom practices; recommend integration with learning management systems, intelligent agents, and faculty training; pilot projects using AI-driven learning analytics.
