# Presidential AI Strategy Task Force

AI Strategy, Coordination, and Governance

Office of the President

## Overview

How should Colorado State University coordinate its institutional approach to artificial intelligence, and where should it accelerate, limit, defer, or decline adoption, so that AI-related work strengthens CSU's land-grant mission while protecting academic integrity, privacy, security, equity, institutional trust, and responsible use of university data?

This initiative uses a phased approach. Phase 1 will develop an institutional philosophy, align existing efforts, identify gaps and decision points, and recommend actions. Phase 2 will translate that framework into governance, implementation, incentives, and operating practices. The goal is not to solve every AI-related issue, but to create a durable approach that lets CSU move with purpose and confidence.

## Objectives by Phase

### Phase 1: AI Philosophy, Coordination, and Strategic Framework

1. Inventory and Align Existing AI Work
2. Define CSU's Institutional Philosophy on AI Across Domains
3. Coordinate Guiding Principles Across Domains
4. Clarify Decision Rights and Risk Boundaries, including Assessing Existing Governance and Policy Structures and Identifying Gaps
5. Recommend the Phase 2 Governance and Implementation Agenda

### Phase 2: AI Operating, Governance, Incentives, and Implementation Plan

1. Vet the Phase 1 Framework with Campus Constituents
2. Define CSU's AI Governance Model
3. Establish Incentives for Adoption, Growth, and Responsible Utilization
4. Connect AI Strategy to Institutional Capacity and Investment

## Timeline

- **Phase 1:** October 2026 to January 2027
- **Phase 2:** February 2027 to May 2027
