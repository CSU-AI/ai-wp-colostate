# How We Use AI on This Site

AI @ CSU helps people at CSU Fort Collins use AI well. We hold this site to the same standard we ask of everyone else: use AI where it helps, check the results, and have people review what we publish.

## What We Use AI For

Staff in the Division of IT use AI tools to:

- Suggest terms and outlines for pages
- Draft and edit text for clarity and plain language
- Check consistency across pages
- Help build and test pages

AI does not decide what this site says. People choose the topics, set the guidance, and approve every page before it is published.

## What We Don't Do

- We don't publish AI output without a person reading and editing it.
- We don't enter confidential, restricted, or student information into AI tools to write or edit pages.
- We don't use AI to write policy. Policy comes from CSU and CSU System governance, and we link to the source.

## How We Review and Disclose

People review everything on this site before it is published, whatever tools helped draft it. A person is responsible for every page.

The footer on every page links here, so this page covers the whole site. Where AI played a significant part in a page, such as drafting definitions or guidance, the page may also carry a short note saying what it did. We follow the Artificial Intelligence Disclosure (AID) Statement format used on the CSU System site, and we leave out model version numbers because they are out of date quickly.

## Check Our Work

AI makes mistakes, and so do people. If you find something wrong, unclear, or missing on this site, tell us at [Contact Us](/contact-form/). We will correct it and, when it affects what a page says, update the page's review date.
