# AI Groups

## Purpose

List the CSU committees, working groups, and communities of practice working on AI. Split out of the Governance page on 2026-11-10.

## Page copy

### Official Committees and Working Groups

- [CSU System Data Governance Steering Committee](https://datagov.csusystem.edu/data-gov-program-charter/) sets guidance, system-level requirements, and principles for CSU data with AI tools.
- [Presidential AI Strategy Task Force](/about/governance/presidential-ai-strategy-task-force/): sets CSU's institutional AI philosophy, coordination, and governance model. Phase 1 runs October 2026 to January 2027.
- [Provost's Artificial Intelligence Working Group](/about/governance/provosts-ai-working-group/): guides how AI is integrated into teaching, learning, advising, and academic operations. Charged in Fall 2026.

### Communities of Practice

- Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education
  - Contact: Joseph Brown, Ph.D., TILT, Joe.Brown@colostate.edu
- [Data Science Research Institute AI-HPC Activity Group](https://www.research.colostate.edu/dsri/dsri-activity-groups/)

### Previous Groups

Two previous CSU System AI groups have completed their work.

- The [Enterprise AI Design Team](/about/governance/previous-groups/) (January through December 2025)
- The [AI Joint Task Force](/about/governance/previous-groups/) (September to December 2024)
