# AI Governance at CSU Fort Collins

## Purpose

Describe campus decision-making and initiatives while directing visitors to CSU System policy and Data Governance for system-level requirements.

## Required content

- Current Fort Collins governance structure and decision owners
- Enterprise AI Design Team charter, membership, and initiatives
- AI Joint Task Force outcomes and completed work
- Current initiative status
- Participation and contact information
- Links to CSU System AI and Data Governance

## Campus AI groups

- [Presidential AI Strategy Task Force](/about/governance/presidential-ai-strategy-task-force/): sets CSU's institutional AI philosophy, coordination, and governance model. Phase 1 runs October 2026 to January 2027.
- [Provost's Artificial Intelligence Working Group](/about/governance/provosts-ai-working-group/): guides how AI is integrated into teaching, learning, advising, and academic operations. Chartered Fall 2026.
- [Previous AI Groups](/about/governance/previous-groups/): the Enterprise AI Design Team (through December 2025) and the AI Joint Task Force (September to December 2024).

## CSU System AI Governance Guidelines

**[AI Governance Guidelines](https://datagov.csusystem.edu/ai-governance-guidelines/) (CSU System)**

Developed by the CSU System Data Governance Committee, this resource outlines institutional guidelines for the ethical, secure, and transparent use of AI across administrative, research, clinical, and educational domains. It emphasizes responsible data stewardship, equity, and human oversight, offering classification-based protocols and principles for AI use.
