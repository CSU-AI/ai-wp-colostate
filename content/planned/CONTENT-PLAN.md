# Content plan

This structure was initialized from David's `ai.colostate.edu-sitemap` project note. It is a working proposal, not an approved navigation model.

## Scope boundary

The site serves the Fort Collins campus. It owns campus tools, teaching, research, training, support, news, and local governance. System policy, legal guidance, data governance, risk methodology, and systemwide bodies are linked from their authoritative sites rather than duplicated.
